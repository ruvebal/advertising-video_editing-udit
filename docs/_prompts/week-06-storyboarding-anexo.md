# Week 6 Annex: Storyboarding Masterclass

### Storyboarding Masterclass (40 minutes)

#### 1. What is a Storyboard? (10 min)

**Definition:**
Visual representation of your edit, shot by shot, before you film.

**Components of Each Panel:**

1. **Visual:** Sketch or description of shot
2. **Shot Type:** Wide, medium, close-up, etc.
3. **Camera Movement:** Static, pan, tilt, dolly, etc.
4. **Duration:** Approximate seconds
5. **Audio:** Dialogue, music, sound effects
6. **Notes:** Special instructions, transitions

**Why Storyboard?**

- **Saves time:** Know exactly what to shoot
- **Prevents mistakes:** Catch problems before filming
- **Communicates vision:** Team understands plan
- **Guides editing:** Roadmap for post-production

**Show Examples:**

- Professional storyboards (Pixar, commercial production)
- Simple stick-figure storyboards (still effective)
- Digital storyboards (apps like Storyboarder)

#### 2. Storyboarding for Advertising (15 min)

**Key Principles:**

**1. Every Shot Has Purpose**

- Establishes location
- Shows product
- Reveals emotion
- Provides information
- Transitions to next beat

**2. Think in Beats**

- Beat 1: Setup (who, where, what)
- Beat 2: Conflict/need
- Beat 3: Product introduction
- Beat 4: Resolution
- Beat 5: CTA

**3. Plan for Editing**

- Coverage: Multiple angles of key moments
- Cutaways: B-roll for transitions
- Inserts: Close-ups of product, hands, details
- Reactions: Faces responding to action

**4. Time Management**

- 30 seconds = roughly 15-20 shots (2 seconds each)
- Or 6-10 shots (3-5 seconds each)
- Depends on pacing desired

**5. Brand Integration**

- When does product appear? (Usually by 10-second mark)
- How is logo shown? (Pack shot, in-use, environment)
- Where is CTA? (Usually final 5 seconds)

#### 3. Common Storyboarding Mistakes (10 min)

**Mistake 1: Too Ambitious**

- Planning 50 shots for 30 seconds
- Complex locations or effects
- **Solution:** Simplify, focus on core message

**Mistake 2: No Coverage**

- Only one angle per moment
- No cutaways or inserts
- **Solution:** Plan wide, medium, close-up for key beats

**Mistake 3: Ignoring Transitions**

- Shots don't connect logically
- Spatial confusion
- **Solution:** Plan how each shot leads to next

**Mistake 4: Forgetting Audio**

- No plan for dialogue, music, or sound effects
- **Solution:** Note audio for each shot

**Mistake 5: Unclear Timing**

- No sense of pacing
- **Solution:** Write approximate duration on each panel

#### 4. Demo: Storyboarding Live (5 min)

**Instructor demonstrates:**

- Take a simple brief: "30-second ad for eco-friendly water bottle, target: college students"
- Choose structure: Problem/solution
- Sketch 8 panels live:
  1. Wide: Student in class, plastic bottle (3s)
  2. Close-up: Plastic bottle in trash (2s)
  3. Medium: Student researching on phone (2s)
  4. Close-up: Product website (2s)
  5. Medium: Student receives package (3s)
  6. Close-up: Product reveal (3s)
  7. Montage: Student using bottle in various locations (10s)
  8. Wide: Student happy, logo + CTA (5s)

**Total: 30 seconds, 8 shots**

### Practical Exercise: Speed Storyboarding (50 minutes)

#### Exercise Overview

**Objective:** Create a complete 30-second commercial storyboard in 30 minutes.

**Challenge:** "Speed Storyboard Sprint"

**Format:**

- Individual work
- 30 minutes to complete
- Present to small group for feedback

#### The Brief (Instructor Provides Options)

**Option 1: Sustainable Fashion Brand**

- Product: Eco-friendly clothing line
- Target: 18-30 year olds, environmentally conscious
- Platform: Instagram Reels
- Message: "Style doesn't have to cost the Earth"

**Option 2: Productivity App**

- Product: Task management app
- Target: Busy professionals, 25-40
- Platform: YouTube pre-roll
- Message: "Get more done, stress less"

**Option 3: Local Coffee Shop**

- Product: Artisan coffee shop
- Target: Local community, all ages
- Platform: TikTok
- Message: "Your neighborhood's best-kept secret"

**Option 4: Fitness Equipment**

- Product: Home workout gear
- Target: 20-45, fitness enthusiasts
- Platform: TV commercial
- Message: "Gym quality, home convenience"

#### Production Phase (30 minutes)

**Step 1: Choose Structure (5 min)**

- Problem/solution
- Emotional appeal
- Slice of life
- Demonstration
- Humor/surprise

**Step 2: Outline Beats (5 min)**

- Write out 4-5 key story beats
- Assign approximate timing to each

**Step 3: Sketch Panels (15 min)**

- 6-12 panels (depending on pacing)
- Simple sketches (stick figures okay!)
- Note shot type, duration, audio

**Step 4: Review and Refine (5 min)**

- Does it tell complete story?
- Is timing realistic?
- Is brand integrated?
- Is CTA clear?

**Instructor Circulates:**

- Answer questions
- Provide feedback
- Encourage efficiency

---

### Small Group Feedback (20 minutes)

**Format:**

- Divide class into groups of 4-5
- Each person presents storyboard (3 minutes)
- Group provides feedback (2 minutes)
- Rotate through all group members

**Feedback Questions:**

- "Is the story clear?"
- "Is the timing realistic?"
- "Where is the product integrated?"
- "What's the strongest moment?"
- "What could be simplified?"

**Instructor Visits Each Group:**

- Listen to presentations
- Offer expert feedback
- Identify strong examples to share with class

---

### Wrap-Up & Videothon Preparation (10 minutes)

**Share Best Examples:**

- Show 2-3 strong storyboards to full class
- Discuss what makes them effective

**Team Videothon Reminders:**

**Week 7 Schedule:**

- **Session A:** Final planning, storyboard refinement
- **Session B:** Shoot and edit (intense 2-hour sprint)
- **End of Session B:** Screen all team videos

**What to Bring:**

- Camera/phone
- Props (if needed)
- Enthusiasm and teamwork!

**Success Tips:**

1. **Plan thoroughly:** Storyboard is everything
2. **Keep it simple:** Ambitious plans fail under time pressure
3. **Assign roles:** Director, camera, talent, editor
4. **Stay flexible:** Adapt when things don't go as planned
5. **Have fun:** This is creative play under pressure

> _"The battle is won before the first shot is fired. The edit succeeds before the first cut is made. Plan well, and you will prevail."_
