Course Overview

This 10-week course explores the art and craft of video editing for advertising, from historical foundations to contemporary digital platforms. Students will master Adobe Premiere Pro while developing a critical understanding of persuasive media.

Assignments

Structured tasks that apply course concepts: analysis of existing work (Weeks 1–4), the capstone persuasive piece, re-edit exercise, and final self-portrait.

Due Week 4

Analysis Assignment

Select one complete piece; deliver an oral analysis (screening + defense + Q&A) and submit a written guide + USB with samples.

Read assignment →
Before Week 5

Capstone: Persuasive Piece

Group project (Weeks 1–4 synthesis): shoot and edit a max-2-min persuasive spot (product or idea; music, no speech).

Read assignment →
Edit 02

Re-Edit (15s or 30s)

Individual re-edit: remake an existing piece into a 15s or 30s spot; apply Week 5 experimental techniques and Week 6 advertising structure.

Read assignment →
Final project · 5 weeks

Self-Portrait (Edit 03)

Individual final project: a self-portrait in video form (inner world, influences, work). Four-layer sound, mixed materials, three platform versions (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok).
