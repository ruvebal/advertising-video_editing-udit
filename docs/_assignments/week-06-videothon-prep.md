# Week 6 Assignment: Team Videothon Preparation

Team assignment preparing for the Week 7 Team Videothon - form teams, receive briefs, and create detailed storyboards.

---

## Overview

| **Element** | **Requirement** |
| ----------- | --------------- |
| **Teams** | Groups of 3-4 students |
| **Deliverable** | Detailed storyboard (6-12 panels) |
| **Goal** | Prepare for efficient Week 7 production |
| **Brief** | Provided in class (product/cause + target audience) |

---

## Phase 1: Team Formation (In Class)

### Team Requirements
- **Size:** 3-4 students per team
- **Skills:** Diverse skill sets recommended:
  - Director (vision, leadership)
  - Camera operator (technical skills)
  - Editor (post-production)
  - Talent/coordination (on-screen presence)

### Team Actions
- Exchange contact information
- Schedule planning meeting (30 minutes)
- Assign roles based on strengths
- Choose team name (optional but fun)

---

## Phase 2: Brief & Concept Development

### Your Team Will Receive:
- **Product or Cause** to promote
- **Target Audience** demographics
- **Key Message** requirements
- **Platform** specifications (TV, social media, etc.)

### Brainstorming Session (30 minutes)
1. **Read brief thoroughly**
2. **Brainstorm concepts** (no bad ideas initially)
3. **Select strongest concept**
4. **Apply classic advertising structure**:
   - Problem/Solution
   - Emotional Appeal
   - Testimonial/Social Proof
   - Demonstration
   - Humor/Surprise

---

## Phase 3: Detailed Storyboard Creation

### Storyboard Requirements

#### Format
- **6-12 panels** total
- **16:9 aspect ratio** boxes
- **Clear visual descriptions**
- **Detailed notes** for each panel

#### Each Panel Must Include:
1. **Visual:** Sketch or detailed description
2. **Shot Type:** Wide, medium, close-up, etc.
3. **Camera Movement:** Static, pan, tilt, dolly
4. **Duration:** Approximate seconds
5. **Audio:** Music, sound effects, voiceover
6. **Notes:** Transitions, special instructions

### Storyboard Template

```
Panel 1: [0-3 seconds]
┌─────────────────┐
│ Visual sketch   │
│ or description  │
└─────────────────┘
Shot: Wide establishing
Camera: Static
Audio: Music begins
Notes: Set the scene
```

---

## Phase 4: Production Planning

### Shot List Planning
- **List every shot needed**
- **Note camera requirements**
- **Plan for coverage** (multiple angles)
- **Schedule filming order**

### Location & Logistics
- **Scout locations** (if needed)
- **Check permissions** for filming
- **Gather props** and costumes
- **Test equipment** beforehand

### Brand Integration
- **Product placement** (when/how it appears)
- **Logo visibility** (pack shot, environmental)
- **CTA placement** (usually final 5 seconds)
- **Brand colors/style** consistency

---

## Individual Practice Assignment

### Personal Storyboard Exercise
While teams work on main project, each student will:

1. **Choose a product** (real or imagined)
2. **Select one advertising structure**
3. **Create a 6-panel storyboard** for a 30-second ad
4. **Bring to Session B** for peer review

### Structure Options:
- **Problem/Solution:** Show need → introduce product → result
- **Emotional Appeal:** Build feeling → connect to brand
- **Testimonial:** Authority figure → endorsement → CTA
- **Demonstration:** Challenge → product in action → proof
- **Humor/Surprise:** Setup → twist → brand connection

---

## Submission Requirements

### Team Deliverable
- **Completed storyboard** (6-12 panels)
- **Shot list** (detailed filming plan)
- **Team contact list** (roles confirmed)
- **Location plan** (if applicable)

### Individual Deliverable
- **Practice storyboard** (6 panels)
- **Structure choice** with brief justification

### Format
- **Digital preferred** (PDF or clear photos)
- **Hand-drawn acceptable** if neat and readable
- **All text must be legible**

---

## Assessment Criteria

### Team Storyboard (10 points)
- **Clarity** (3 points): Can anyone understand the plan?
- **Completeness** (3 points): All required elements included
- **Creativity** (2 points): Original concept within brief
- **Practicality** (2 points): Can this be filmed in Week 7?

### Individual Storyboard (5 points)
- **Structure understanding** (2 points): Correctly applies chosen format
- **Visual thinking** (2 points): Clear shot descriptions
- **Branding integration** (1 point): Product/brand appears appropriately
