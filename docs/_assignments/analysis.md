# Analysis Assignment

## Goal

- **Discern persuasive and conviction logics** in a single complete work.
- **Comprehend and apply** all concepts introduced in **Weeks 1–4**: platform and persuasive editing, Lumière/Méliès in-camera choices, continuity and Gestalt, and Soviet montage (Kuleshov, juxtaposition, rhythm).

---

## Piece selection

- Choose **one complete piece** (advertisement, short film, music video, PSA, branded content, or other finished work).
- **Complete only** — no excerpts or clips as your primary object of analysis. The piece must be viewable in full so the class can follow your argument.
- Any format/length is acceptable as long as it is a single, self-contained work.

---

## Oral presentation (in class)

| Part                         | Time              | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Screening**                | **Max 3 minutes** | Show the piece. You may show the full piece and then speak, or screen key moments within the 3-minute limit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Defense (analysis pitch)** | **5 minutes**     | Oral analysis: techniques, persuasive vs. convincing logic, ethical considerations, and explicit links to course concepts (Weeks 1–4). Use the **film editing terms glossary** from **Lesson 1** ([NYFA Film Editing Vocabulary](https://www.nyfa.edu/student-resources/a-beginners-guide-to-film-editing-vocabulary/)) to name techniques precisely and to **impulse critical thinking** — why cuts work, for whom, and with what effect. |
| **Q&A**                      | **2 minutes**     | Questions from instructor and class.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

**Total per student:** **10 minutes** (3 min screening + 5 min defense + 2 min Q&A).

---

## Supporting materials (both mandatory per defense)

### Written guide (mandatory)

- Submit a **written guide** (structured notes, outline, or short essay) that supports your oral defense.
- **Resume notions more than authors:** focus on **concepts and techniques** (continuity, montage, Kuleshov, Gestalt, persuasion vs. convincing, etc.) rather than citing directors or theorists. Summarize _what_ the piece does and _how_ it uses editing; use the [NYFA Film Editing Vocabulary Guide](https://www.nyfa.edu/student-resources/a-beginners-guide-to-film-editing-vocabulary/) to keep language precise and to support critical reflection.
- Reference: Kuleshov Effect, Gestalt principles, continuity vs. montage, and ethical dimensions of the piece.

### USB with samples (mandatory)

- **Screen a reconstruction or loops** (e.g. key moments, edited highlights, or a reassembly) during your presentation to illustrate your points.
- **Bring a USB drive** with all presentation files ready (video file(s), correct format/codec for the classroom setup). Mandatory for every defense so playback is reliable and examples are available.

---

## Concepts to reference (Weeks 1–4)

Your analysis must show that you can identify and discuss:

| Week  | Concepts                                                                   |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1** | Platform, medium, persuasive editing, McLuhan                              |
| **2** | Lumière (reality), Méliès (trick/transition), in-camera choices            |
| **3** | Continuity editing, 180° rule, match on action, Gestalt principles         |
| **4** | Kuleshov Effect, montage, juxtaposition, rhythm, persuasion vs. convincing |

Address both **how** the piece is cut and **why** — i.e. how editing supports persuasive or conviction-based logic.
