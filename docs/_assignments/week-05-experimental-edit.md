# Week 5 Assignment: Experimental Edit

Individual assignment creating a 30-second experimental edit that intentionally breaks conventions while remaining purposeful.

---

## Overview

| **Element** | **Requirement** |
| ----------- | --------------- |
| **Length** | 30 seconds (±5 seconds) |
| **Technique** | Must use at least ONE experimental technique |
| **Goal** | Break rules with purpose, not chaos |
| **Music** | Optional but encouraged for rhythm |
| **Concept** | Clear emotion or message |

---

## Assignment Options

Choose ONE approach for your experimental edit:

### Option A: Jump Cut Montage

- **Subject:** "A Day in the Life" or "Getting Ready"
- **Technique:** Use jump cuts to compress time
- **Approach:** Remove all "boring" moments
- **Style:** Fast-paced, energetic

### Option B: Nonlinear Narrative

- **Subject:** Tell a simple story out of order
- **Technique:** 3-5 shots rearranged for effect
- **Challenge:** Make it comprehensible despite non-linear structure
- **Style:** Artistic, intriguing

### Option C: MTV-Style Music Video

- **Music:** Choose 30 seconds of music
- **Technique:** Rapid cuts synced to beats
- **Style:** Stylized transitions and effects
- **Goal:** Maximum energy and rhythm

---

## Technical Requirements

### Footage Gathering

- Shoot new footage OR use stock footage
- Variety of shots for experimentation
- Bring all footage to editing session

### Editing Techniques (Choose at least ONE)

#### Jump Cuts
- Cut within same shot/scene
- Create visible discontinuity
- Remove pauses, "ums", dead space

#### Speed Ramping
- Dynamic speed changes
- Slow motion emphasis
- Fast-forward compression

#### Glitch Effects
- Digital distortion
- RGB splitting
- Pixelation/datamosh

#### Whip Pans & Smash Cuts
- Fast motion transitions
- Abrupt scene changes
- High energy cuts

---

## Submission Requirements

### Video File
- **Format:** H.264, 1080p
- **Length:** 30 seconds (±5 seconds)
- **Audio:** Music optional, no dialogue required

### Written Component (150-200 words)

Answer these questions:
1. **What technique did you use and why?**
2. **How does the rule-breaking serve your concept?**
3. **What audience/brand would this work for?**
4. **What would you refine with more time?**

### Title Card
Include at beginning:
- Your name
- Option chosen (A, B, or C)
- Technique(s) used

---

## Assessment Criteria

### Technical Execution (3 points)
- ✓ Experimental technique applied correctly
- ✓ Clean execution (no technical errors)
- ✓ Appropriate length and pacing

### Creative Concept (4 points)
- ✓ Clear purpose for rule-breaking
- ✓ Technique serves emotion or message
- ✓ Original and bold approach
- ✓ Demonstrates understanding of when to break rules

### Effectiveness (2 points)
- ✓ Comprehensible despite experimentation
- ✓ Engages viewer (not boring or confusing)

### Polish (1 point)
- ✓ Proper export and submission
- ✓ Title card and written component
