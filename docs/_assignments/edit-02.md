# Edit 02: Re-Edit a Piece (15s or 30s)

Re-edit an existing piece into a tight advertising-style cut at a fixed duration:

- **15 seconds** (platform-native / high-compression)
- **30 seconds** (classic spot / clearer arc)

Your goal is to apply:

- **Week 5 paradigms:** rule-breaking with purpose (jump cuts, nonlinear compression, rhythm-first cutting, glitch as meaning, etc.)
- **Week 6 paradigms:** advertising structure + platform constraints (hook, beats, branding, CTA)

---

## Overview

| **Element**                  | **Requirement**                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Format**                   | Re-edit (do not shoot a new film from scratch)                                                   |
| **Duration**                 | Choose **exactly one**: **15s** OR **30s**                                                       |
| **Core constraint (Week 6)** | Clear hook + readable structure + branding + CTA                                                 |
| **Rule-breaking (Week 5)**   | Use at least **one** purposeful experimental technique                                           |
| **Audio**                    | Music allowed; voiceover allowed only if you already have it in the source or can record cleanly |
| **Deliverables**             | Video export + short written reflection                                                          |

---

## Inputs

Use the piece provided by the instructor (or the agreed source footage from class). You may:

- Remove / reorder / replace shots from within the source
- Add titles / supers / a CTA card
- Replace music and rebuild rhythm

Do not solve the task by “making a completely new film.” This is a **re-edit**.

### Step 1: Choose your duration track (pick one)

### Track A: 15 seconds

- Treat this as **platform-native compression**.
- **Hook:** first **1–2 seconds** must clearly establish the idea.
- **Brand/CTA:** must land in the **final 3 seconds**.

### Track B: 30 seconds

- Treat this as a **classic 30-second spot**.
- **Hook:** first **2–3 seconds**.
- **Brand/CTA:** must land in the **final 5 seconds**.

### Step 2: Apply Week 6 advertising structure (choose one)

Pick one structure and make it readable in your cut:

- **Problem → Solution → Result → CTA**
- **Emotional appeal montage → Brand meaning → CTA**
- **Testimonial / social proof → CTA**
- **Demonstration / proof → CTA**
- **Humor / twist → Brand connection → CTA**

### Step 3: Apply Week 5 experimental editing (choose at least one)

Use at least one technique **on purpose** (it must serve meaning, rhythm, or emotion):

- **Jump-cut compression** (time removal, staccato energy)
- **Nonlinear rearrangement** (re-order beats for reveal)
- **Rhythm-first cutting** (music-driven, montage logic)
- **Smash cuts / whip-pan style transitions** (contrast and momentum)
- **Glitch / distortion as a signal** (memory, tech, rupture, intensity)

### Hard requirements

- **Exact duration:** export must be **exactly 00:15** or **exactly 00:30**.
- **Branding:** visible brand moment (logo/pack/brand name) at least once.
- **CTA:** one clear call-to-action in the final seconds (text is fine).
- **Legibility:** if you use text overlays, they must be readable at full speed.

### Deliverables

### 1) Video export

- **Codec:** H.264
- **Resolution:** 1080p
- **Filename:** `edit-02_LASTNAME_15s.mp4` or `edit-02_LASTNAME_30s.mp4`

### 2) Written reflection (100–150 words)

- Which **Week 6 structure** did you use?
- Which **Week 5 technique** did you use, and what did it communicate?
- Where are your **hook** and **CTA** (timecodes)?

### Assessment criteria (10 points)

- **Structure clarity (Week 6) (3 pts)**
- **Purposeful rule-breaking (Week 5) (3 pts)**
- **Hook + CTA execution (2 pts)**
- **Timing discipline: exactly 15s/30s (1 pt)**
- **Polish: audio levels, readability, clean export (1 pt)**
