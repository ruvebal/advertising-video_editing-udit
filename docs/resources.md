## Books, Articles & Academic References

A consolidated list for deeper reading and citation. All DOIs link to the canonical article or standard.

### Books

**Editing & film theory**

- **Walter Murch** – *In the Blink of an Eye* — Editing hierarchy, emotion-first.
- **Michael Ondaatje** – *The Conversations* — Interviews with Walter Murch.
- **Sergei Eisenstein** – *Film Form* — Montage and meaning.
- **David Bordwell & Kristin Thompson** – *Film Art: An Introduction* — Continuity, 180° rule, classical Hollywood style.
- **Michael Frierson** – *Film and Video Editing Theory* (Routledge, 2018) — How editing creates meaning; synthesizes Zettl, Eisenstein, Murch, Dmytryk.

- **[Murf AI](https://murf.ai/)** — Professional voice-over generator with 200+ AI voices and multiple accents, widely used for explainer videos and corporate narration.

## Community Best Practices

{% include tao-quote.html quote="The wise editor organizes before creating. Chaos in the project folder leads to chaos in the timeline." %}

### Project Organization
```
PROJECT_NAME/
├── 01_RAW/
│   ├── Video/
│   └── Audio/
├── 02_SEQUENCES/
├── 03_EXPORTS/
├── 04_ASSETS/
│   ├── Music/
│   ├── SFX/
│   └── Graphics/
└── 05_ARCHIVE/
```

### Naming Conventions
- Use descriptive names: `SCENE01_INTERVIEW_JOHN_V02.mp4`
- Include version numbers
- Use dates in ISO format: `2025-01-14`

### Backup Strategy
- 3-2-1 Rule: 3 copies, 2 different media, 1 offsite
